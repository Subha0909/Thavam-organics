About Sesame Oil

Sesame oil (gingelly oil) is one of the oldest edible oils, traditionally extracted from sesame seeds using the cold-press (marachekku) method. It has a distinctive nutty flavour and is rich in antioxidants, healthy fats and essential minerals like copper and calcium. Widely used in South Indian cooking, Ayurvedic body massage and traditional wellness practices, sesame oil is valued both as a superfood and a natural skincare ingredient.
