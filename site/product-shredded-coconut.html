Shredded Coconut

Freshly shredded coconut flakes, ideal for cooking, garnishing and traditional recipes.

  • Color: Natural White

  • Usage: Cooking, Garnishing, Chutneys
  • Shelf Life: Best used fresh / 7-10 days refrigerated

About Shredded Coconut

Shredded coconut is prepared by finely shredding fresh coconut kernel, keeping its natural moisture and flavour intact. It's widely used in South Indian cooking for chutneys, curries and garnishing sweets, adding a rich, fresh coconut taste to every dish.
